Before you begin

Essential
questions.

Your access, your data and what is available today.

What is Open Model Router?

A model-routing layer designed to choose an appropriate model for a request using configurable preferences, rather than tying every task to one default model.

Is it another AI model?

No. It is a routing layer between your client and the models it may use, not a new foundation model.

How is a model selected?

The decider considers the request, your configured preferences and eligible models. Direct mode uses your explicit choice.

Is my subscription enough?

A subscription does not automatically provide API access usable by OMR. Access depends on each provider’s supported methods.

What does “local” mean?

The router runs on your machine. A remote model receives the data needed for the request; local does not mean offline. Context sent to an external decider must be explicitly configured and consented to. Configured local commands are trusted code, not isolated from the system.
